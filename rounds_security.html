Security and Privacy

How Rounds handles your data, where it lives, and what the app will never do.

Data flow
What happens when you record a round
1
Audio goes to Whisper for transcription
Your voice recording is sent over HTTPS to OpenAI Whisper through a Cloudflare proxy. The audio is transcribed to text and discarded. Rounds never stores your audio anywhere.
2
Text goes to Claude for filing
Only the text transcript reaches Claude, not audio. Claude reads it, sorts it into your four document types, and returns structured data. The transcript is not stored server-side.
3
Filed data saves to your device
The structured result comes back to your phone and is saved locally. From this point forward, it never leaves your device. No cloud backup, no sync, no server.

How the app is built
Security by design
Zero keys on your phone
All API keys live on a Cloudflare Worker, encrypted at rest. Your phone never sees them. Even if someone had your device, there are no keys to find.
Every request is authenticated
A shared secret header authenticates your app to the server on every request. No valid header, no access to the AI services.
No accounts, no passwords
There is no Rounds account to hack. No email and password combo. No login screen. Your data exists only on the device in your hand.
Delete everything in one tap
Settings > Delete All Data. Two confirmation steps, then it's gone. Every round, every note, every setting. Wiped permanently.
Third-party services
Only two services touch your data

Rounds uses exactly two external services, both solely to power the core functionality. Neither is used for tracking, analytics, or advertising.

Service What it receives Purpose
OpenAI Whisper Audio (discarded after transcription) Speech to text
Anthropic Claude Text transcript only Filing into documents

No analytics SDKs. No ad networks. No tracking pixels. No data brokers.

What Rounds will never do
Hard rules, not suggestions

These are built into the architecture. They can't be toggled on or quietly changed in an update.

✕
Never store your audio
Audio is processed in-session and discarded. There is no audio archive, no playback feature, no recordings sitting on a server.
✕
Never upload your data to a Rounds server
There is no Rounds server. Your filed data lives on your phone. Future sync features will be opt-in and transparent.
✕
Never share your information with third parties
No selling data, no marketing partnerships, no "anonymized" data sharing. The only services that see anything are the two AI providers that make the app work.
On the roadmap
Team accounts are coming, on your terms

Rounds is currently single-device with no accounts and no cloud storage. That's intentional for v1. Team features are coming, and they follow the same principles.

→
Team accounts
Managers invite reps to a shared workspace. Authentication handled by AWS Cognito. Industry-standard, encrypted, no passwords stored by Rounds.
→
Cloud sync (opt-in)
Filed rounds sync across devices and to the team dashboard via AWS. Encrypted in transit and at rest. Solo reps can keep everything on-device if they prefer.
→
Same privacy rules apply
No data selling, no third-party sharing, no analytics tracking. The infrastructure changes. The principles don't.
