ROUNDS

Field sales documentation,
done out loud.

Rounds is a voice-first iOS app for outside sales reps. Tap the mic, speak your stop notes, and the app files everything into structured documents while you're still in the parking lot.

How it works
Speak it. File it. Roll.
1
Tap the mic and talk
After each stop, tap one button and speak your notes naturally. No forms, no typing. Just talk about what happened.
2
AI transcribes and files
Whisper converts speech to text. Claude reads the transcript and sorts every detail into the right document. Visit logs, follow-ups, weekly recap, scouted leads. Facts stay locked as spoken.
3
Review and roll
Cards appear instantly. Confirm a status tag on each stop with one tap, make any quick edits, and move on. The whole process takes less time than walking back to the truck.
Four documents, one recording
Everything files from a single voice note

Each recording is sorted into four document types automatically. You never have to think about where something goes.

Round
Today's visit log. Every stop, locked as spoken. Who you saw, what happened, what's next.
Open
Active follow-ups. Unresolved items roll forward day to day until they're closed.
Recap
Weekly manager snapshot. Stops, wins, blockers. One page, ready to send.
Scout
New leads and scouted locations. Territory intelligence captured on the road.
Why it matters
Notes get lost. Follow-ups get forgotten.
Eight stops, one steering wheel, zero time to type. By the end of the day, half the details are gone. The name of the manager at stop three, the part number from stop five, the callback you promised at stop seven.
English is now the interface. LLMs made it possible to speak naturally and get structured output. This architecture was not economically viable two years ago.
Form-based CRMs can't replicate this. Competitors are built around forms at the core. Bolting voice onto a form-based system produces a voice-to-form tool, not a voice-first filing system.
Everything stays on device. No cloud accounts, no syncing, no data sharing. Audio is processed in-session and never stored. You own your data.
