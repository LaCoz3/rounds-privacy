Privacy Policy

Last updated — March 30, 2026

Rounds is a voice-first field sales app built for outside sales representatives. This policy explains what data the app handles, how it is processed, and what rights you have over it.
01
What Data Rounds Handles

Profile information — your name, territory, and manager email address, entered during onboarding and stored on your device.

Field notes — voice recordings and transcribed text you create during rounds. This includes business names, contact names, visit summaries, and follow-up notes.

App preferences — your chosen appearance setting and onboarding state.

02
How Audio Is Handled

When you record a round, your audio is sent to OpenAI's Whisper API for transcription. The audio is transmitted securely through a Cloudflare Worker proxy and is not stored on any server controlled by Rounds. OpenAI may retain audio for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring purposes under their standard API terms. No audio file is stored by Rounds after transcription is complete.

The transcribed text is then sent to Anthropic's Claude API to organize your notes into structured document types. Only the text transcript reaches Claude — no audio is involved at this stage. Anthropic's standard API data handling terms apply.

03
Where Your Data Is Stored

All filed rounds, open items, scout leads, calendar events, and profile information are stored locally on your device. This data does not leave your device except as described in the audio handling section above. There is no Rounds account, no Rounds server, and no cloud backup.

04
Third-Party Sharing

Rounds does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party for marketing or advertising purposes. The only third-party services that receive any data are OpenAI (audio transcription) and Anthropic (text filing), both used solely to provide the core features of the app.

05
Data Deletion

You can permanently delete all data stored by Rounds at any time. Go to Settings and tap Delete All Data. This removes all rounds, open items, scout leads, calendar events, and profile information from your device immediately. This action cannot be undone.

06
Children

Rounds is designed for professional use by adults. The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

If this policy changes materially, the updated date at the top of this page will reflect the change. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
