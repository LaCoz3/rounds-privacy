Privacy Policy

01
What data Rounds handles

Profile information. Your name, territory, and manager email. Entered during setup, stored on your device.

Field notes. Voice recordings and transcribed text from your rounds. Business names, contact names, visit notes, follow-ups.

App preferences. Your appearance setting, onboarding state, and theme choice.

02
How audio is handled

When you record a round, the audio goes to OpenAI Whisper for transcription. It's sent through a Cloudflare Worker proxy over HTTPS. Rounds does not store your audio anywhere. OpenAI may retain it up to 30 days under their API terms.

The text transcript then goes to Anthropic Claude to sort into your four document types. Only text reaches Claude, not audio. Anthropic's standard API terms apply.

03
Where your data lives

Everything (filed rounds, open items, scout leads, calendar events, profile) is stored on your device. It does not leave your phone after filing. There is no Rounds account, no Rounds server, and no cloud backup.

04
Third-party sharing

Rounds does not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone for marketing or advertising. The only outside services that see any data are OpenAI (transcription) and Anthropic (filing). Both are used only to make the app work.

No analytics. No ad networks. No tracking pixels. No data brokers.

05
Deleting your data

Go to Settings > Delete All Data. Two confirmation steps, then everything is gone. Every round, every note, every setting. Wiped from the device permanently. Can't be undone.

06
Children

Rounds is built for working professionals. It is not directed at anyone under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

07
Changes to this policy

If anything here changes, the date at the top gets updated. Using the app after a change means you accept the new version.
